CNG's pitch is simple: it's usually priced well below petrol. But "cheaper per kg" isn't the same question as "worth switching to," and the second question depends on a few things the price tag alone doesn't tell you.

## The running-cost case is usually strong

CNG is typically priced significantly below petrol, and a well-installed kit doesn't cost as much mileage as the price gap would suggest — most vehicles retain a large share of their petrol efficiency on CNG. For anyone doing meaningful daily distance, this combination usually means a real, ongoing saving per kilometre, not a marginal one.

## What the sticker price doesn't include

Three things determine whether that saving is actually worth capturing:

- **Conversion cost.** If you're converting an existing petrol vehicle rather than buying CNG-ready, the kit and fitting cost is real money upfront. The per-km saving has to earn that back before it's a net win — which depends entirely on how much you drive.
- **Boot space and cylinder placement.** The CNG cylinder takes up real space, usually in the boot. For some vehicles and use cases (family trips with luggage, commercial use needing cargo room) this is a genuine trade-off, not just a footnote.
- **Station availability.** CNG infrastructure is far less widespread than petrol pumps outside a handful of cities. If your daily routes don't pass near a CNG station reliably, the running-cost advantage is undermined by the practical cost of detours to refuel.

## Who benefits most

The math tends to favour CNG most clearly for:

- High-distance daily drivers (commercial use, long commutes) where the per-km saving accumulates fast enough to justify a conversion cost.
- Vehicles in cities with genuinely good CNG station coverage along normal routes.
- Owners planning to keep the vehicle long enough for the conversion cost to be fully offset.

It's a weaker case for low-mileage drivers, or anyone in an area where CNG stations are sparse enough that refuelling becomes a planning problem rather than a routine stop.
